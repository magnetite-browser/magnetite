## Shipped with the app (macOS, Windows, Linux)

- **CEF and Chromium**: CEF 156.0.26 (commit `8ea0c73`) with Chromium
  156.0.8078.88. That is the CEF library (`Chromium Embedded Framework.framework`
  on macOS, `libcef.dll` on Windows, `libcef.so` on Linux) and the files next
  to it (`.pak` resources, `icudtl.dat`, the V8 snapshot, SwiftShader and the
  Vulkan loader). CEF is BSD-3-Clause (above). Chromium and its ~490
  third-party components carry their own licences, listed in `CREDITS.html` of
  the CEF distribution, which ships next to this file. Most are permissive.
- **Copyleft code inside the CEF library.** It contains code under the LGPL
  (FFmpeg and libusb, LGPL-2.1-or-later; Blink's WebKit-derived code,
  LGPL-2.0/2.1), the MPL (for example symphonia, Eigen, hunspell, and
  Mozilla's URL parser and certificate code) and, on macOS, the Apple Public
  Source License. Magnetite ships the CEF library unmodified, as a separate
  dynamic library. Its corresponding source is Chromium 156.0.8078.88
  (<https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/refs/tags/156.0.8078.88>)
  with CEF commit `8ea0c73b5dbed1389c8dafb911a98c41246bb8cb`
  (<https://github.com/chromiumembedded/cef/tree/8ea0c73b5dbed1389c8dafb911a98c41246bb8cb>).
  For at least three years after we last distribute this version, we give
  anyone who asks a copy of that source, for no more than the cost of
  distribution. Ask through the project's issue tracker.
- **Windows only.** `d3dcompiler_47.dll` is a Microsoft binary, redistributed
  under the Windows SDK redistribution terms. It is not open source and not
  listed in `CREDITS.html`. `dxcompiler.dll` is the DirectX Shader Compiler
  (University of Illinois/NCSA licence, in `CREDITS.html`).
- **Rust standard library**, compiled into every Rust binary: MIT OR
  Apache-2.0, used under MIT, plus a few bundled crates under their own
  permissive licences. The full notices are the Rust toolchain's
  `COPYRIGHT-library.html` (`$(rustc --print sysroot)/share/doc/rust/`), which
  ships next to this file.
- **Rust crates and npm packages**: the generated tables and licence texts
  below. Some contain more than their licence field says: `ring` contains
  BoringSSL code (Apache-2.0 and ISC), `webpki-roots` carries Mozilla's CA
  list under CDLA-Permissive-2.0, and `lucide-react` is ISC with some icons
  under MIT (derived from Feather). Their texts are below.
- **Sidebar assets**: the sidebar uses system fonts and Lucide icons only. The
  app icon is the project's own work, under the EUPL-1.2.

CEF's `resources.pak` also carries the credits and the CEF licence
(`chrome://credits`, `chrome://license`).

The sync protocol (`magnetite-sync-proto`) is our own code from the
magnetite-sync repository, under the EUPL-1.2 like this one; the sync server
carries its own notices there.

## Not shipped

- **Widevine CDM** (proprietary, Google). Magnetite does not redistribute it. On
  first use, CEF's Chrome runtime downloads it from Google through Chromium's
  component updater, which is a connection to Google servers. Streaming
  services that require Verified Media Path only work with a VMP-signed app,
  and that signing needs an agreement with Google (Widevine).
- **H.264 and AAC.** Stock CEF builds ship without these proprietary codecs.
  Adding them needs our own CEF build and a decision on patent licensing.
